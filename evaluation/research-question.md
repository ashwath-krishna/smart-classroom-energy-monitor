# Research Question

## Main question

Does combining mmWave radar and PIR sensing with conservative vacancy logic reduce false-vacancy decisions compared with a PIR-only approach under the same classroom conditions?

## Supporting questions

- How does each approach behave when a person is sitting still, reading, or writing?
- How do the approaches respond when a person enters or leaves?
- Can moving objects such as a running fan cause unwanted occupancy detections?
- How long does each approach take to declare a room vacant?
- How closely do the energy estimates agree with an independent reference reading, if such a reference is available?

## Hypothesis to evaluate

The dual-sensor approach may reduce false-vacancy events, but it may also increase false-occupancy events or require additional tuning. The experiment should measure both types of error.
